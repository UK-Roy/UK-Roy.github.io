## BRAC University

**Lecturer, Department of Computer Science and Engineering**  
Dhaka, Bangladesh

- **CSE461: Introduction to Robotics**  
  Covered fundamental concepts including robot kinematics (forward and inverse), motion planning, and mobile robot navigation.

- **CSE461: Robotics Laboratory**  
  Designed and conducted weekly hands-on laboratory sessions focusing on practical robotics workflows, tools, and system integration to bridge theory with real-world applications.

- **Student Supervision and Mentorship**  
  Supervised undergraduate robotics projects, conducted tutorials, and provided structured office-hour mentoring to guide students through problem-driven implementations.

## Workshops and Training

- **ROS 2 Workshop (2023)**  
  Department of Robotics & Mechatronics Engineering, University of Dhaka  
  Delivered hands-on training on ROS 2 architecture, simulation, visualization tools, and programming for both simulated and physical robots.

- **NAO Robot Workshop (2022)**  
  Department of Robotics & Mechatronics Engineering, University of Dhaka  
  Conducted a one-day workshop introducing students to programming and operating the NAO humanoid robot.

## Mentorship and Outreach

- **Mentor, Bangladesh Robot Olympiad (BdRO)** (2018–2023)  
  Mentored competitive robotics teams annually, with teams achieving **silver and bronze awards** at the International Robotics Olympiad (IRO).

- **Judge, National Science Carnival (2023)**  
  Served as a judge for the Line Following Robot segment at Dhaka Residential Model College (DRMC).

My teaching philosophy emphasizes **strong theoretical foundations combined with hands-on experimentation**, encouraging students to develop intuition, discipline, and confidence in building real robotic systems.
